Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Tuner ("the app") is a chromatic instrument tuner for iOS. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Microphone Access

The app requests access to your device's microphone solely to detect the pitch of sounds in real time. Audio is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any information with third parties because no information is collected in the first place.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.

Contact
